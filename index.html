Manual

Swarm — show one index finger and move it around.

Duel — needs two hands; particles split into two swarms following each index finger.

Battle ⚡ — needs two hands. Pinch to charge, release to fire; show index + middle fingers to shield.

Pinch — boosts that hand's gravity 3×, particles glow gold.

Supernova 💥 — in Duel mode, bring hands close together to detonate.

Beat-Drop 🎵 — load music or use mic; particles pulse to bass and beats.

Combo — chain supernovas, beats and kamehamehas to build combo!
